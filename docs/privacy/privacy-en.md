**In short.** Version 1.0 has no accounts, no server, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter is
stored only on your device. We don't receive, see or sell your data.

## What you enter

- Profile: name or nickname, sex, age, height, weight, activity level, your twin's look.
- Logs: food, water, steps, workouts, body measurements, weigh-ins, goals.
- Game data: points, pet, achievements.

Weight, food and body measurements are health data. That's why they stay on your device and the app does
not send them anywhere.

## Where the data is stored

- In the app's protected storage on your phone. Other apps can't access it.
- If your phone's system backup is on (Google on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the app's data may be included
  in that backup in your account. You control it in your phone settings; we have no access to it.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the phone.

## Files you create yourself

- A **backup** and a **trainer report** are files you create by hand, and you decide where to send or
  save them. A report covers the period you choose. It includes food logs only if you turned that on.
- Once you send a file (email, messenger, cloud), that service's rules apply.

## Internet and permissions

The app does not send your data over the internet and uses no third-party tracking services. File picking
is used only to restore a backup or open a report.

## Your rights

All the data is with you. You can:
- view and edit it in the app;
- export it to a file (Profile → Backup);
- erase everything with "Erase all data" or by uninstalling the app.

Because we don't process your data, we can't provide or delete it on request. You can complain to the Czech
data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (www.uoou.cz).

## Health

The app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Weight and calorie figures are estimates.
If you are ill, pregnant, have an eating disorder, or for children and teenagers, talk to a doctor.

## Changes

If later versions add accounts, sync or AI, we'll update this policy. Health data will be processed on a
server only after your separate, explicit consent.
